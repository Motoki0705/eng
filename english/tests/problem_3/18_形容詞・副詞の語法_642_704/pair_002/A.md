# 問題3 - 18_形容詞・副詞の語法 - pair_002 - A

**次の各文の空所に入れるのに最も適切なものを、①〜④の中から1つ選びなさい。**

---

**(a)** He doesn't eat ( ) meat because he's trying to lose weight.

① many  
② few  
③ much  
④ a few  

---

**(b)** ( ) students came to the farewell party for the retiring professor.

① Quite a few  
② Quite a little  
③ Quite few  
④ A quite few  

---

**(c)** Not a few employees were opposed to the new policy.

① Only a few  
② Very many  
③ Very few  
④ Not very many  

---

**(d)** The lecture was so ( ) that many students fell asleep.

① bore  
② bored  
③ boring  
④ boringly  

---

**(e)** The children found the magic show very ( ).

① amuse  
② amused  
③ amusing  
④ amusingly  

---

**(f)** Students should be ( ) toward their elders.

① respectable  
② respected  
③ respectful  
④ respective  

---

**(g)** Many fish were still ( ) when we opened the net.

① living  
② alive  
③ lively  
④ live  

---

**(h)** I met an old friend on my way ( ).

① to home  
② for home  
③ at home  
④ home
