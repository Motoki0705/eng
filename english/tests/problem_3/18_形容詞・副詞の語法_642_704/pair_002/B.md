# 問題3 - 18_形容詞・副詞の語法 - pair_002 - B

**次の各文の空所に入れるのに最も適切なものを、①〜④の中から1つ選びなさい。**

---

**(a)** There isn't ( ) time left before the deadline.

① many  
② few  
③ much  
④ a few  

---

**(b)** ( ) people have complained about the noise from the construction site.

① Not a few  
② Not a little  
③ Not few  
④ A not few  

---

**(c)** Quite a few tourists visited the ancient temple last month.

① Very few  
② Only a few  
③ A great many  
④ Almost no  

---

**(d)** The documentary was very ( ), so I learned a lot.

① amuse  
② amused  
③ amusing  
④ amusingly  

---

**(e)** I was ( ) by the long speech and couldn't wait for it to end.

① bore  
② bored  
③ boring  
④ boringly  

---

**(f)** He is a ( ) member of the community.

① respectful  
② respected  
③ respectable  
④ respective  

---

**(g)** The city has a very ( ) nightlife with many clubs and restaurants.

① alive  
② living  
③ live  
④ lively  

---

**(h)** She stopped by the bookstore on her way ( ) from work.

① to home  
② for home  
③ at home  
④ home
